APP NAME (25/30)
Arrowmallow: Arrow Puzzle

SHORT DESCRIPTION (74/80)
Tap arrows to slide them off the board. A calm puzzle that's easy to love.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Arrowmallow is a calm, satisfying puzzle game. Tap an arrow and it slides off the board in the direction it points. But if its path is blocked, it bumps into another arrow and you lose a heart. Find the right order, clear the board and earn all 3 stars!

★ HOW TO PLAY
• Tap an arrow to send it off the board
• Arrows always move the way their head points, even the bendy ones
• Bump into another arrow and you lose a heart
• Big board? Pinch to zoom and drag to look around

★ FEATURES
• Endless levels that get harder little by little: bigger boards, longer arrows, more twists
• A "boss" level every 10 levels for when you want a real challenge
• Daily Challenge: a new board every day; keep your streak going for bonus coins
• 8 arrow skins: Warm Pastel, Classic, Candy, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Neon, Gold
• Hints when you're stuck, and gentle reminders so you never lose your streak
• No timers, and you can play offline
• Soothing background music and soft haptics
• Available in 12 languages

Arrowmallow is made for the moments you want to unwind: a few minutes on the bus, a lunch break, or right before bed. Download it and free your first arrow today!
